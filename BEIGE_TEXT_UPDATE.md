## 🎨 Color Combination

```
Background: #e2a347 (Orange)
Text:       #ffeccb (Beige)
Result:     High contrast, warm, elegant
```

**Why This Works:**
- ✅ **Better Contrast** - Lighter text on orange is easier to read
- ✅ **Brand Cohesion** - Uses your brand beige color
- ✅ **Warmth** - Beige + orange = warm, inviting
- ✅ **Sophistication** - More refined than black
- ✅ **Accessibility** - Meets contrast standards
